مصحف المدينة النبوية
Script of the Madinah Mushaf (1441 AH), written by Uthman Taha and published by the King Fahd Complex for the Printing of the Holy Qur'an. Page layout follows the 15-line, 604-page print.

Welcome to the Mushaf

  • Click the page margins or press ← → to turn pages.
  • Swipe, or tap the edges of the screen, to turn pages.
  • Click any verse to highlight it.
  • Press and hold a verse to highlight it. Tap the page to show or hide the controls.
